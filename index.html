How it works

Every step has an AI doing the work and a rule deciding whether money moves.

  1. 01Sponsor funds a goalPayPal checkout. The money is held until it's earned.
  2. 02The Tutor teachesA Gemini tutor works through each milestone, then claims the student is ready.
  3. 03The Examiner tests that claimA second AI asks follow-up questions and quotes the student's own words as proof.
  4. 04Money moves, with a receiptGuardrails approve the release, and every step is logged in a tamper-evident record.
  5. 05An agent buys what's missingThe Study Kit Agent shops for the gaps the Examiner found, within a spending limit the sponsor set.
